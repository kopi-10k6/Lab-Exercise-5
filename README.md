# UX Project Scenarios – Problem Statements, Research Methods & Personas

## Scenario 1: Pixlora – Beauty E-Commerce App 

### Project Description

**Pixlora** is a beauty e-commerce application that allows users to discover, compare, and purchase skincare, makeup, haircare, and other beauty products. The app aims to provide personalized product recommendations and a simple shopping experience.

---

## 1. Identifying and Writing Problem Statements

### User Pain Points

* Users find it difficult to choose suitable beauty products.
* There are too many products and brands to compare.
* Users may not understand product ingredients.
* Finding products according to individual needs takes time.
* Users want personalized recommendations.
* Checkout should be quick and simple.

### Problem Statement 1

> **Beauty shoppers need a way to find products suitable for their needs because they are overwhelmed by the large number of beauty products available, which results in confusion and longer decision-making time.**

### Problem Statement 2

> **Online beauty shoppers need a way to quickly compare product details because they have difficulty understanding differences between similar products, which results in uncertainty when making a purchase.**

---

## 2. Identifying Appropriate Research Methods

### Suitable Research Methods

| Research Method         | Purpose                                | Why It Is Suitable                                    |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| **User Interviews**     | Understand users' shopping experiences | Helps identify difficulties and expectations.         |
| **Surveys**             | Collect information from many users    | Useful for understanding common shopping preferences. |
| **Usability Testing**   | Test the Pixlora interface             | Helps identify navigation and checkout problems.      |
| **Competitor Analysis** | Study similar beauty apps              | Helps identify useful features and UX improvements.   |

### Recommended Methods

**1. User Interviews**

Interviews can be conducted with beauty shoppers to understand how they search for products, compare brands, and decide what to purchase.

**2. Usability Testing**

Users can be asked to perform tasks such as:

> "Find a skincare product → View details → Add to cart → Complete checkout."

This helps identify problems in the actual user flow.

**3. Surveys**

Surveys can collect information from a larger number of users about preferred beauty categories, shopping frequency, recommendations, and important product information.

### Justification

A combination of **interviews, surveys, and usability testing** provides both qualitative and quantitative insights. Interviews explain **why** users face problems, surveys show **how common** the problems are, and usability testing identifies **where** users struggle in the interface.

# Scenario 2: Health Appointment Booking App 

### Project Description

The Health Appointment App allows patients to find doctors, check availability, book appointments, receive reminders, and manage their appointments online.

---

# 1. Identifying and Writing Problem Statements

### User Pain Points

* Patients spend time searching for available doctors.
* Appointment availability may not be clear.
* Patients may need to wait at the hospital.
* Rescheduling appointments can be difficult.
* Users may forget appointment dates.
* Emergency or urgent appointments can be difficult to find.

### Problem Statement 1

> **Patients need a way to quickly find and book available doctors because they have difficulty knowing which doctors and time slots are available, which results in wasted time and unnecessary hospital visits.**

### Problem Statement 2

> **Patients need a way to easily manage and receive reminders for appointments because they may forget appointment dates or need to change their schedule, which results in missed or delayed consultations.**

---

# 2. Identifying Appropriate Research Methods

| Research Method       | Purpose                               | Why It Is Suitable                             |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| **User Interviews**   | Understand patient experiences        | Reveals frustrations with appointment booking. |
| **Surveys**           | Collect feedback from many patients   | Identifies common booking problems.            |
| **Usability Testing** | Test appointment-booking flow         | Finds navigation and interaction problems.     |
| **Diary Study**       | Track appointment-related experiences | Helps understand problems over time.           |

### Recommended Methods

**1. User Interviews**

Patients can be interviewed about their experiences with hospitals and online appointment systems.

**2. Usability Testing**

Users can be given a task:

> "Find a dermatologist → Select an available slot → Book the appointment."

Their actions can be observed to identify usability issues.

**3. Surveys**

Surveys can determine how often users book appointments online and what features they expect.

### Justification

**Interviews** help understand patient needs and frustrations, while **surveys** provide broader data. **Usability testing** is important because healthcare applications should have simple and clear navigation, especially when users may be stressed or in a hurry.
